WHAT'S IN THIS VERSION:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Clean, Modern & User-Friendly Presentation UI.
2. Books Search Bar: Instant search and filtering of all 66 Bible books
   with New Testament (NT) and Old Testament (OT) category filter tabs.
3. VerseView Songs & Hymns Module:
   - Authentic Malayalam Christian worship songs & English Hymns with
     Malayalam lyrics and English/Manglish transliteration.
   - Search by Song Number (e.g. #1, #12), Title, or Lyrics keywords.
   - Stanza-by-stanza 1-click projection (Chorus, Stanzas 1 to 4).
   - "+ Add Song" tool to easily import and save custom church songs.
4. Dual / Malayalam Only / English Only Display Modes.
5. Official Church of God Alappuzha Town branding, watermarks & Welcome Screen.

HOW TO RUN ON ANY LAPTOP / PC:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Copy this folder to your church presentation laptop (e.g. Desktop or C:\ChurchView).
2. Double-click "Launch_ChurchView_Projector.bat".
   - 100% OFFLINE: All 31,102 Scripture verses and songs are stored locally.
   - Works on any Windows 10 or Windows 11 PC (runs automatically with Python or built-in PowerShell).
3. Keep the black launcher window open during church service.

HOW TO PRESENT IN CHURCH:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. On the presenter dashboard, click "OPEN PROJECTOR (O)" in the top header.
2. Drag the projector window across to your Church TV / Projector screen.
3. Press "F11" (or double-click anywhere on the projector window) to go Full Screen!
4. Use the header tabs to switch between "📖 SCRIPTURES" and "🎵 SONGS (VerseView)".
5. Click any verse or song stanza to project it live with 0ms delay!

KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- "M"                 : Toggle between Scriptures (📖) and Songs (🎵)
- "W"                 : Toggle Alappuzha Town Default / Welcome Screen
- "B"                 : Toggle Blackout (Blank screen to pure black)
- "→" or "↓" or Space : Next Verse / Next Song Stanza
- "←" or "↑"          : Previous Verse / Previous Song Stanza
- "Enter"             : Quick Jump / Project selected item
- "O"                 : Open Audience Projector Window
- "/"                 : Focus Quick Search Bar
- "F11" or "F"        : Full Screen (on projector window)

CUSTOMIZING THE DEFAULT SCREEN:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
In the presenter window under "Display Settings", click:
"✏️ Edit Default Screen Text & Logo" to customize church name, motto, theme colors, or upload custom logos anytime!
